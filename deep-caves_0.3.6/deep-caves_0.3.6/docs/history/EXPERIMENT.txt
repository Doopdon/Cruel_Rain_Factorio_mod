Deep Caves 0.2.2 — configurable network meshes

Remove the older Deep Caves ZIP. Keep Cave Walls installed. Change the six network controls under Settings > Mod settings > Startup, restart Factorio when prompted, then create a new world to compare layouts.

Network       Density    Width (tiles)
Large            1            12
Small            4             3
Web             10             1

Density is approximate nodes along 256 tiles: 1 means 256-tile spacing, 4 means 64-tile spacing, 10 means about 26-tile spacing. Higher density creates more nodes and tunnels. Each layer is a separate connected mesh; intersections join the three networks. Width is nominal tile width; diagonal bends and junctions may be wider.

Both automatic hole filling during terrain generation and the old save repair script/command have been removed. Old passage-widening and legacy boundary stitching are no longer invoked. Ramp access paths remain, so entrances can reach the cave mesh. Entrance artwork is rendered on the floor beneath the character.

Enemy density is configurable from 0–10, default 0.1. Values >=1 all mean a 100% initial-nest roll. Changing enemy density updates explored caves gradually. /cave-populate-enemies retries placement. Five dark levels and the flooded final cave remain.

Changing terrain settings does not redraw existing terrain. Use new worlds for comparisons. Restore the stable Deep Caves 0.1.32 and your previous save to revert the experiment.
