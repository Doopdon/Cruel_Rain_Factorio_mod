# Cave Walls 0.1.11
Factorio 2.0. No other mods required. Install cave-walls_0.1.11.zip in your mods folder and load a new or existing save.

Use the stone-wall icon in the shortcut toolbar (enable Cave wall brush in the shortcut selection menu if hidden), or Alt+W. Left-drag paints a rectangle of cave-wall terrain. Shift+left-drag or right-drag restores the original terrain, including paving and underlying hidden tiles. Paint has no cost or research requirement. Q clears the cursor. Strokes are limited to 65,536 tiles and generated chunks. The brush does not place ghosts or assign robot construction jobs.

Cave walls look black, block ground walking/building, and destroy construction, logistic, combat and capture robots, moving projectiles (including rockets), and artillery shells whose ground position enters or crosses them. Normal robot death effects/events apply; bots and carried cargo may be lost. Both personal and roboport robots are covered, including modded robots with those native entity types. Players and ground entities receive no scripted damage. Painting does not delete existing structures, resources or characters. Avoid painting yourself into a wall; erase it if you do. Paved terrain restored by erasing keeps its original hidden base tile.

This is a death barrier, not robot pathfinding: robots still select ordinary routes and can keep crashing into a wall. Player/vehicle instant bullets, other hitscan attacks, beams/lasers, handheld/vehicle fluid streams and splash effects are not blocked by this version. Standard flamethrower turret streams are now blocked. Turrets using the three standard magazine types now fire blockable bullets. Acid or other attacks represented by moving projectile entities are intercepted. Deep Caves 0.1.25 or later integrates these walls into cave generation and converts its existing cave voids. This mod alone does not generate caves or convert native voids.

Invisible, indestructible ground collision helpers cover exposed wall edges (including diagonal edges), so even a one-tile wall stops walking. Solid interiors do not receive helpers. Erasing removes obsolete helpers and exposes neighboring wall edges immediately. Helpers do not block bot flight; the script kills bots on contact. Deep Caves and brush edits synchronize immediately. Background editor/script discovery visits one generated chunk every half second, so discovery latency grows with the number of generated chunks.

The script checks active supported flying entities every tick only on surfaces containing cave walls; it follows the segment between consecutive positions so a fast robot cannot skip a narrow wall. This first experimental version scales with the number of supported flying entities on affected surfaces. Moving shots that spawn and finish their entire flight between checks cannot be intercepted; instant hits are outside this version’s scope. It scans for walls on initialization/update and discovers editor/script-created walls on previously unmarked surfaces in its incremental chunk sweep. Brush painting activates checks immediately. Generators can call remote.call("cave-walls", "sync_area", surface.index, area) after terrain edits to activate walls and remove obsolete collision helpers immediately; register_surface performs a full surface synchronization. Wall contact uses the bot's center projected onto the terrain, not its sprite height or shadow.

Erase restores tiles saved by this brush. Walls created by other tools/scripts have no saved original and erase to stone-path. Undo is the erase gesture; native Ctrl+Z is not supported for strokes. Painted terrain and restore records persist through saving/loading. Erase walls before uninstalling if you want the original terrain restored.

Validated in Factorio 2.0.76: load/create, paint/erase and hidden terrain restoration, narrow wall walking/build collision, construction/logistic bot deaths, fast and diagonal crossings, combat bot coverage, save/load, and a real autonomous bot charging flight across a wall.

0.1.1 fixes the missing brush/shortcut icon by bundling its own 64-pixel PNG. No gameplay changes.

0.1.2 fixes paint/erase selection events: use their surface object instead of indexing game.surfaces with a nonexistent surface_index.

0.1.3 expands contact checks to combat/capture robots, projectiles and artillery projectiles. Intercepted shots disappear without applying their impact/explosion payload. This does not provide general line-of-sight checks or prevent splash damage from impacts on open terrain. Existing walls automatically acquire the new behavior.

Native 2.0.76 checks for this release cover all base robot types, regular/explosive rockets, artillery shells, a fast projectile crossing a one-tile wall, stable projectile tracking across saving/loading, safe shots, and no impact damage at the target behind the wall.

0.1.4 fixes enemies becoming permanently excluded from Space/turret targeting after a targeted projectile is intercepted. Factorio 2.0.76 retains predicted incoming damage after script destruction of the projectile. This release disables predicted-death targeting suppression for health-bearing prototypes, including modded ones; existing affected enemies become targetable when the updated mod is loaded. Actual health and damage are unchanged. The tradeoff is that weapons may fire surplus shots while lethal shots are still in flight. This affects targeting throughout the world, not just near cave walls.

Native 2.0.76 regression: an entity-targeted rocket is intercepted; the affected biter survives Space-style pistol fire under 0.1.3, including after saving/loading. Loading that same affected save with 0.1.4 restores auto-targeting and kills the biter. Existing robot, rocket, artillery and fast-shot barrier checks still pass.

0.1.5 replaces the turret-only hit effects of firearm, piercing and uranium magazines with small moving bullet projectiles (0.8 tiles/tick, 48 tiles/second). Each projectile carries the original ammo impact/damage effects; the turret does not also inflict an instant hit. Muzzle flashes and sounds, magazine consumption, range, firing cooldown, ammo damage bonuses and turret damage bonuses remain native. These projectiles can be intercepted by cave walls. A creation trigger records their starting position so the first movement segment is checked too. Existing turrets and ammunition update on loading the mod; no rebuilding or new recipes are needed. Player/vehicle magazine effects, lasers and flamethrowers are unchanged. The previous predicted-damage targeting workaround remains enabled.

Native Factorio 2.0.76 checks: all three standard turret magazine types damage open targets with the correct combined ammo/turret research bonuses, consume ammunition, and deal zero damage across one-tile walls; diagonal and near-target walls also block shots. Turrets resume damaging an affected target when its wall is erased. Player and vehicle magazine deliveries remain instant. Bullet creation tracking is exercised by actual turret fire.

0.1.6 adds cave-wall interception for the standard flamethrower turret. Native flame streams are retained on clear paths, including fuel consumption, crude/heavy/light-oil bonuses, research, particle visuals, stickers, ground fire, targeting and firing sounds. A firing-source callback records the muzzle and a creation callback uses the native stream midpoint to reconstruct the intended landing position. Streams whose ground path crosses a cave wall are canceled before ignition; blocked shots still consume fuel. Existing tracked streams are rechecked so painting a wall during a burst cancels them too. Erasing a wall restores ordinary firing. This is stream cancellation, not a jet visibly splashing against the wall.

Scope: standard flamethrower turret only; handheld/tank flame streams and lasers remain unchanged. Fire already burning on the ground, its spread, and splash effects are not given line-of-sight filtering. Installing/updating removes any old native turret flame streams still in flight once; it does not remove existing ground fires. Existing turrets update without rebuilding.

Native Factorio 2.0.76 checks pass for all three oils, open-path burning, zero damage/ground fire across blocked stream paths, a diagonal wall, erased-wall firing resumption, and painting a wall while native streams are actively in flight (old ground fire/stickers cleared in that fixture to isolate interception). The gun-turret bullet regression also passes with this release.

0.1.7 adds pre-firing cave-wall sight checks to standard gun turrets. When their current target is behind a wall, they choose a clear hostile military target within their quality-adjusted range. Fallback selection respects target priority order and the ignore-unprioritised setting, then chooses the nearest target of equal priority. If no clear target is available, the mod temporarily pauses the turret to avoid spending ammunition. Current target paths are checked every tick; paused guns search for new alternatives six times per second. They resume automatically when a target moves into a clear path or walls are erased. Turrets already inactive before this mod's intervention are left alone. Bullet interception remains a fallback for shots already in flight and changing terrain. This applies to the standard gun-turret, not modded gun turret prototypes. Flamethrower streams retain 0.1.6 behavior: blocked streams are canceled but shots still consume oil.

Native Factorio 2.0.76 regression: blocked-only gun turrets expend zero magazines or individual rounds; a turret with a blocked nearer target attacks a clear alternative; inactive turrets stay inactive; ignored unprioritised targets are not shot; erasing walls and moving targets into view restore firing. All three magazine impact/research checks and the flamethrower stream checks also pass with 0.1.7.

0.1.8 applies the pre-firing sight check to all three standard turrets: gun, laser and flamethrower. Turrets select clear hostile military targets within their quality-adjusted range, with flamethrower minimum-range and firing-arc checks. Target priorities and ignore-unprioritised settings are retained. Turrets pause when only blocked targets are available, conserving magazines, oil and laser shot energy, and resume when targets become visible or walls are erased. Checks also run on initialization/configuration changes, player/robot/script building, and brush painting/erasing. Existing inactive turrets remain inactive. Native firing effects remain on clear paths; handheld/vehicle weapons and modded turret prototypes are unchanged. Unlike 0.1.6/0.1.7, flamethrower turrets now hold fire instead of continually spending oil on canceled streams. Laser standby power is still normal; shots/beams and ground fires already emitted before terrain/target movement changes are not rewound, and an existing laser beam may finish its brief lifetime. Projectile and flame-stream interception remain fallback protection.

Native Factorio 2.0.76 combined regression passes for all three turret types: zero blocked-target damage; no rounds or oil expended against static blocked targets; no laser shot-energy expenditure; clear alternative targets hit; inactive turrets preserved; erasing walls restores damage. Laser test turrets are supplied energy every tick to isolate aiming from lack of power.

0.1.9 adds the `sync_area(surface_index, area)` remote API for Deep Caves integration. Terrain edits synchronize only the affected area, including removing obsolete collision helpers when a wall becomes floor. The brush and 0.1.8 combat behavior remain available in standalone worlds.

0.1.10 fixes cave-scale performance: collision helpers are restricted to exposed wall boundaries, the repeating whole-map wall scan is replaced with a single-chunk background sweep, and standard turrets use a persistent registry instead of a whole-surface search every tick. Normal player/robot/script-raised turret build events register turrets immediately; generators synchronize edited areas through the remote interface. Direct script-created turrets without build events are discovered during the background sweep. Flying entity checks still run every tick, preserving fast crossing interception. Loading an existing save automatically removes obsolete interior helpers once without rerolling terrain or altering inventories. That first cleanup/load can take longer on large caves, but it is not repeated every ten seconds. Keep Deep Caves 0.1.25 installed; no cave restart is needed.

## 0.1.11 performance diagnostic switch

- `/cave-walls-checks off`: disables all Cave Walls script checks across every level, including per-tick flying interception, flame-stream filtering, turret sight checks, periodic terrain sweeps, immediate generator/brush synchronization, and collision-helper creation. Only turrets paused by this mod are reactivated. Existing wall tiles and collision helpers remain in the native engine, so an unchanged result does not rule out their engine cost. Deep Caves generation remains active.
- `/cave-walls-checks on`: re-enables checks and performs a one-time wall/turret resynchronization. This command can pause on a large map; compare steady performance after it completes.
- `/cave-walls-checks status` (or no argument): prints the saved switch state, wall surface/helper counts, turret registry size and sweep chunk count.

Changes require an admin and persist in the save. Checks default ON. Bots and shots may pass through walls while OFF, and edits may leave collisions stale until re-enabled. Turning checks back on preserves terrain, ramp inventories, and turrets that were deliberately inactive. Data-stage ammo/stream prototypes and predicted-damage targeting changes remain loaded; this is a runtime diagnostic switch, not uninstalling the mod. Use it while standing still in the same area to isolate runtime cost; also try moving into a new area while OFF to distinguish generation spikes. Keep Deep Caves 0.1.25 and replace only Cave Walls with this update. No world restart is required.


## 0.1.12 timing diagnostics

Run `/cave-walls-diagnostics start 10` while the game is lagging. After 10 simulation seconds, a report prints average milliseconds per tick for wall-helper synchronization, flying-object searches/crossing checks on each surface, turret discovery, turret line-of-sight/targeting, and flame-stream checks. It also counts synchronized wall tiles and flying objects processed. `stop` prints early; `status` prints helper/flying-object counts per wall surface. Samples support 1–60 whole seconds and end on reload. Only admins start/stop samples.

Wall sync includes calls from Deep Caves. Timing never changes gameplay. There is no profiling timer allocation outside a requested sample. Native entity collision/pathfinding work between script events is outside the report; if timings are small but the game is slow, also capture F4 -> show-time-usage. The existing `/cave-walls-checks off` switch leaves native collision helpers in place, so disabling the mod and pausing its scripts are different comparisons. No world restart needed.


## 0.1.13 event-based flying checks

Native robot deployments and projectile/artillery creation now register entities through preserved/extended prototype creation effects. Each tick checks only tracked entities, not the entire entity list on every surface. Existing objects are discovered once after an upgrade or checks re-enable, one surface per tick. Newly painted wall surfaces also get a one-time discovery. There can be brief first-load discovery spikes, but these scans do not repeat. Tracked references persist in saves, invalid references are discarded, cross-surface moves do not create false long-distance traces, and existing creation effects remain intact. The same exact tile crossing logic intercepts fast shots. Diagnostics now label the one-time work as Flying discovery search and ongoing work as Tracked flying wall crossing. No new world required.

The visual entities do not add extra collision or selection boxes; they are just there to make cave boundaries read like raised rock instead of flat black terrain.
