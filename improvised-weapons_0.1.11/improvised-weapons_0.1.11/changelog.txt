Date: 2026-10-07

Version: 0.1.8
Date: 2026-10-07
  Features:
    - Added generated barbed-wire walls (80 health, 4 physical damage/second).
    - Added generated electric-fence poles/walls (180 health, 40 electric damage/second, 20 kW draw).
    - Electric fences connect together and supply nearby machines.
  Changes:
    - Spinners damage player characters and apply a small collision-checked knockback.
    - Added one coal to each basic, piercing, and uranium bullet-magazine recipe.
    - Gun-turret recipes require five green circuits and turrets require 10 kJ/shot plus 1 kW idle power.
    - Flamethrower-turret recipes require ten green circuits and turrets require 50 kW electricity as well as fluid ammunition.
    - Gun-turret technology requires red, green, and military science.
